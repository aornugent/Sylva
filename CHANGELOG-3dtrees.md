# 3dtrees changes to Sylva

What our `develop` adds over upstream (`git log main..develop`), as it stands: rewritten as `develop` changes, until we version. `main` mirrors upstream.

- `qsm.support_tree` (with `SupportFlag`) is the joint model's fast path for one tree. Each row's pulses, per scan, reduce to a profile across it whose edges and front depth measure the support function of its cross-section, linear in a leaning circle's centre, lean and radius; a stop counts as wood by its echo character (`JointParams.refl_*`); a row longer than 30 cm is measured in pieces combined into its line. Per row a weighted least squares over its scans, then one exact linear solve over the tree with a prior after `joint_tree`'s: the taper's steps and the base's level, at a fork the child's share of the subtree wood length (not of the prior area), the centre's and lean's continuity along a branch, the centre's anchor and a weak lean anchor, no tip anchor. Rows one circle can't summarise (two or more stems in a profile, a hull or misfit wider than one circle, too few edges, no opaque run) are flagged for `joint_tree`, and their radius is the prior's. It exists because `joint_tree` takes minutes per large tree and the plot needs about half a CPU-second per tree: on Alice Mulga tree 849 it takes 0.6 CPU-s against 323, and on the rows it measures agrees with the refit's measured radii better than RCT does; but it flags about 80 % of rows, so it can't yet stand alone. `JointParams` gains the `refl_*` fields and goes to version 4 (version 3 records are refused); `joint_tree`'s outputs are unchanged. Additive.
- `develop` is upstream `main` (v0.3.2 and its Zenodo DOI) without `Shots.from_scan`, which 3dtrees replaced: upstream's `fill_missing_from_timing` recovers misses from pulse timing, so 3dtrees sorts each export itself and calls `from_pointcloud`. Changes no outputs.
- `qsm.evidence` checks each cylinder of a QSM against the scan rays: the radius its echoes measure and how precisely, the beam calliper, the pulses that pass inside the claimed radius, and how far pulses penetrate, with the hits' reflectance and deviation. RCT's radii are mostly a taper prior, and this says which ones the rays support, contradict or can't see, and tells leaf clumps fitted as fat twigs from wood. `qsm.ScanIndex` bins each scan's pulses by direction, so a tile's cylinders are checked in seconds, and keeps each echo's return number and each pulse's number of returns where the store has them. Additive.
- `qsm.pairs` returns the raw table that `qsm.evidence` reduces inside: every pulse near each cylinder (a tube radius per cylinder, or one for all) that reached its tube, with its closest approach to the axis, all its echoes (range, reflectance, deviation, return number), its number of returns and whether the export deleted some of its echoes (`lost`); `ScanIndex.origin` gives the scanner position. The joint model is a per-pulse likelihood and needs that table, not evidence's summaries. Additive.
- `qsm.joint_tree` (with `JointParams` and `JointReason`) fits the joint wood, foliage and ray model to one tree in one call: its rows and its neighbours' cylinders in, a fitted row per stem out, with each row's radius interval, posterior shares, existence (`log_odds`, `p_wood`, `reason`) and the split of clumps into solid stems, decided per row by evidence against a BIC price (`split_gain`). Each row is fitted alone first, then the tree with per-row and per-branch step control; each stop is owned by the row whose surface explains it. 3dtrees' screen and candidates call it per tree, as they call `evidence`. `JointParams` is checked (its version, exactly its fields) and is what a cache of fits keys on. A new function: changes no existing output.
- `qsm.joint_fit` and `qsm.joint_table` are `joint_tree`'s parts, for research: the pair table (passes where a deleted echo could lie in the chord are kept and flagged `deleted`, each echo the export deleted placed between its recorded neighbours by return number), and the fit of a table, saddle-free Newton with a projected line search over a Gaussian Markov prior (taper, soft Leonardo at forks, a tip anchor, the centre's and foliage's random walks, per-(scan, branch) offsets) and an optional radius cap per row. The Newton system is solved exactly as a tree of branch blocks, leaves first, and block sums are deterministic. It returns the Laplace summary (from the true Hessian where it is negative definite: `exact`), each row's share of its own rays (`rho_local`), its likelihood message and an η profile. Additive.
- `qsm.joint` is the joint model's per-pulse likelihood in Rust, with its exact gradient and Hessian from one evaluation on dual numbers; `_core.qsm_joint_loglik` exposes it, stateless, for checking. The likelihood needs wood wide enough to give the share τ of the footprint, with a detection edge of sd `edge` and mixed pixels; `hide` makes a pass by a pulse that lost an echo a mixture with a deleted stop. It adds a `libm` dependency (already in the lock) for erfc. Additive.
- Parity cases for the joint model's likelihood and pair table (`tests/parity/cases_joint.py`), recorded against 3dtrees' numpy oracle, so a change to either is a recorded change. Tests only.
- `synthetic.scan(..., solids=)` puts solid, opaque capped cylinders in a beam scan beside the points: a sub-beam stops at their surface and its echo takes the attributes of a point that stands for them. Wood drawn as surface points echoes off its back face and has its thin silhouette widened by up to 60 %, so the 3dtrees bench couldn't score wood under 2 cm. Scans without solids are unchanged.
- `io.tile_ray_clouds` cuts Shots files into buffered raycloudtools ray-cloud tiles, streamed with bounded memory. `rayextract` can't fit a whole plot in memory, and its input has to keep each echo's ray back to its scanner. Beside each cloud, sidecars in cloud order name every ray's echo: `scan.u16` (its file), `pulse.u32` (its pulse's row) and `echo.u8` (its place in the pulse), so what `rayextract` writes per ray joins to the echo and its pulse exactly. `cloud.ply` is byte-identical, but the tiles are keyed on `ray_tiles.rs`, so each plot re-tiles once, rebuilds its RCT graphs cold and rescreens; verdicts carry over, since they key on structure.
- `qsm.surface_support` and `qsm.surface_distance` measure how well points sit on a QSM's cylinder surfaces; the 3dtrees viewer colours points with `surface_distance`. Additive.
- Docstrings for `qsm.evidence.contradicted`, `qsm.surface_distance` and `ScanIndex.n_pulses`, so upstream's `test_public_api_is_documented` passes on `develop`. Docs only.
